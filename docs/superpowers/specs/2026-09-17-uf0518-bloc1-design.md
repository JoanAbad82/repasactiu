# Repàs Actiu — UF0518 Bloc 1 Integration Design

**Date:** 2026-09-17

## Goal

Incorporate as new course content the uploaded PDF **`1. La comunicació escrita cartes comercials i documents administratius (1).pdf`**, corresponding to **UF0518 — Gestió auxiliar de la correspondència i paqueteria a l’empresa, Bloc 1: La comunicació escrita, cartes comercials i documents administratius**.

The integration must preserve the existing UF0517 bank unchanged except where generic audit infrastructure must become count-independent. No UF0518 question may be created from knowledge that is not supported by the uploaded PDF.

## Academic source boundary

The uploaded 28-page UF0518 PDF is the only academic authority for the new block.

Questionable or absent material must not be completed from general knowledge, the web, legislation, or other training materials.

The question bank may use content actually developed in the PDF, including:

- definition, purpose and practical value of written communication;
- sender, receiver, message, channel and code;
- differences in communication practice between public and private organisations, only where the PDF discusses document/formality/record-keeping aspects;
- written-language functions: referential, appellative, expressive, phatic and metalinguistic;
- clarity, precision, concision, correctness, coherence and cohesion;
- linguistic register and treatment;
- common errors and linguistic recommendations;
- commercial letters: purpose, formality and structure;
- heading, recipient, reference, subject, date, greeting, body, closing, signature, annexes and postscript;
- order, offer, complaint and collection letters;
- administrative documents developed by the material: oficio, memorandum, circular and instance/request;
- memorandum vs circular;
- structure and quality criteria of a request;
- circular purpose and recommended structure.

The UF0518 objectives on reception, classification, registration, distribution, packaging, shipping methods or telematic correspondence do **not** by themselves authorize questions when the concept is not developed in Bloc 1.

Bibliography, title/closing slides and pure illustrative examples may support context but must not become standalone factual questions unless the underlying concept is taught explicitly.

## Coverage audit before question writing

Before writing questions, create a page/topic coverage audit of the UF0518 PDF.

Each teachable concept must be classified as:

- `COVER`: deserves at least one question;
- `COMBINE`: better tested together with a closely related concept;
- `EXCLUDE`: title page, bibliography, duplicate explanation, objective-only item, or content too incidental to justify a reliable test item.

The audit determines the final number of questions. The expected density is roughly **70–80 questions**, but this is not a quota. Quality, non-duplication and source support take precedence over reaching a number.

Questions should vary between direct concept recognition, short practical cases, distinctions between similar concepts, identifying document parts, and selecting the appropriate document or communication criterion. Avoid trivial rewordings of the same fact.

## Question contract

Every new UF0518 question must have:

- a stable ID using `uf0518-b1-NNN`;
- exactly four non-empty, semantically distinct answer options;
- exactly one correct answer;
- Catalan canonical content;
- Spanish translation of question, topic, four options and explanation;
- the same logical correct answer in both languages;
- a concise explanation grounded in the PDF;
- one bilingual memory aid with type `example` or `idea`;
- memory aid maximum of 144 Unicode characters per language.

Distractors must be plausible enough to require understanding, but cannot rely on facts absent from the PDF.

## Data architecture

Follow the existing Repàs Actiu separation of concerns:

- canonical Catalan question bank under `site/data/`;
- Spanish indexed translation under `site/data/i18n/es/`;
- bilingual memory aids under `site/data/memory/`;
- catalogue registration in `site/data/course.json`.

UF0518 should start as one canonical block bank unless file size or maintainability clearly requires a supplemental bank. Do not split purely to imitate UF0517 history.

Existing correction machinery remains available, but new questions should be correct in their canonical bank rather than relying on `content_corrections.json` from the outset.

## Audit architecture

The current certification infrastructure must be generalized so future question counts are derived from the catalogue rather than hard-coded to `450`.

Requirements:

1. **Question-bank validation** derives the published total from all registered blocks.
2. **Bilingual contract audit** validates every published question, not an expected fixed count.
3. **Permutation audit** runs all 24 option orders for every question in Catalan and Spanish; expected case count is `questionCount × 24 × 2`.
4. **Source traceability** keeps UF0517 and UF0518 academically distinct. Preserve the existing UF0517 traceability record and add a UF0518-specific manifest tied only to the uploaded PDF.
5. **Audit summary** reports totals per formative unit and the combined total.
6. Any missing translation, memory aid, trace entry, duplicate option, invalid correct index, orphan supplement or out-of-scope source causes CI failure.

The historical UF0517 audit result remains valid as a historical certification; the new combined audit supersedes it for the currently published site.

## Source traceability for UF0518

Create a UF0518 traceability manifest that covers every `uf0518-b1-NNN` ID.

Trace entries must identify at least the PDF and a page or page range narrow enough to justify the question. Broad `1–28` mappings for all questions are not acceptable.

Pages containing the relevant explanation and tables/diagrams may be cited. The rendered page must be considered when layout or table structure carries meaning that parsed text alone may lose.

## Testing strategy

Use TDD for infrastructure and integration changes.

Required automated coverage:

- RED test proving existing validators reject the unregistered/missing UF0518 contract or still assume 450;
- catalogue/load test for the new UF and block;
- data validation for all new questions;
- bilingual completeness and logical-answer alignment;
- memory-aid 144-character enforcement;
- exhaustive 24-permutation × 2-language audit for all published questions;
- source-traceability completeness with narrow page ranges;
- regression proving all pre-UF0518 question IDs and correct indices remain unchanged;
- E2E showing UF0518 on the home screen, starting a UF0518 quiz, answering in Catalan, switching to Spanish, and receiving correctly aligned feedback/explanation/memory aid.

Run the full `npm run audit:full` before merge.
