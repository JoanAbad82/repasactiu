# Security Policy

## Scope

Repàs Actiu is a static study application without accounts or an application backend.

## Reporting

Do not open public issues containing credentials, private student data, private source mappings, answer-key material, or other sensitive information. Report sensitive security concerns privately to the repository owner.

## Data model

Progress, theme, language, and study state are stored locally in the browser. The application does not provide server-side account storage or cross-device synchronization.

## Content and source safety

- Do not commit private course material, substantial source dumps, answer keys, or private traceability mappings.
- Treat external suggestions and generated content as untrusted until verified against official teaching material and repository validators.
- Do not weaken content/traceability validators merely to make a proposed change pass.

## Limitations

Repàs Actiu is an educational aid. It may contain errors; published content and generated study aids should be reviewed against the available official material.
